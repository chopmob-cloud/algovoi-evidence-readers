## What S-10 does and does not evidence

S-10 is the case AlgoVoi contributed to the contract. Its agreement here is a
self-consistency check, not independent evidence: we wrote the case and we wrote
this reader, so the two agreeing says the case is implementable as stated, nothing
more. The other ten were written by someone else, and agreement on those remains a
check by an outside implementation.

The case itself covers a branch the contract names but the corpus did not
exercise: a dispatched attempt whose `result.preimage.outcome` is not completed.
Deleting that branch from the contract's own `run_contract.py` still scored 10 of
10 before S-10 was added.

## What this record does not establish

- Not a blind result, for the reason above and for the reason in
  `RUN-2026-11-01.md`: the reader was written with the contract text in hand,
  which is what the contract asks for, and it is directed by that text.
- One interpreter on one host. The earlier record used CPython 3.12 and 3.11 in
  two clean-room rounds each; this one did not.
- Nothing about any consumer of the contract. This is the contract read by an
  outside implementation, not the contract driving anyone's SDK.
