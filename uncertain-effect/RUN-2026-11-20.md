## What the revision change caught

The corpus went from 10 ordered checks over 27 cases to 15 over 54 between
`c588c8a` and `6ba08cb`. Run unchanged against the new revision, the reader of
1 November answered **54 of 54 and disagreed on 13**. The thirteen fall into four
families, and naming them is the point of this section:

| Family | Cases | What the reader was missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `worker_crashed` was not treated as a lost reply | 4 | The contract names three replies that leave the outcome unknown. The reader listed two, so a crashed worker read as a settled `not_run` or a duplicate |
| The trigger-key check (check 5) | 3 | An attempt a redelivery, replay or completion set off was not required to carry the operation's own key, so a key spelled after the task identifier passed |
| The ledger-accounting check (check 8) | 2 | `ownerEffects` was compared only against one, never against the number of attempts that could have produced an effect |
| The stop checks (checks 13 to 15) | 4 | The `stop` block was not read at all: authority accepted after a revocation, an effect committed wholly after the stop reported quiescent, and an effect straddling the acknowledgement all passed |

All five new predicates were then written from the corpus README's ordered list,
in the corpus's own order, and the existing checks renumbered to it. No check
outside the stated fifteen was added, and no reason outside the corpus's own
vocabulary is emitted.

  the first version, and the five new predicates were written after reading the
  corpus's stated checks. The run shows the checks are implementable by a second
  pair of hands; it does not show the text is determinate from a cold start.
- **One interpreter on one host.** The 1 November record used CPython 3.12 and
  3.11 in two disposable Linux containers. This run did not, so the second
  interpreter and the container replay are outstanding rather than passed.
- **Nothing about producer honesty, the owner's ledger being correct, or clocks.**
  A record is what someone wrote down.
- **Nothing about the receipt-binding case.** Those four axes are absent here,
  not passed.
